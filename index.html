DOCUMENTATION & SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Is FryPDF really 100% offline?

Yes. FryPDF has zero telemetry, zero analytics beacons, and no network sockets. All document parsing, AcroForm computations, and PDF export occur on your local CPU.

What is the .frypdf file format?

.frypdf is a lossless JSON-based project schema that stores full vector coordinates, live LaTeX formulas, and AcroForm binding graphs without rasterizing your document.

How does FOIA redaction work?

Unlike superficial black highlighter boxes, FryPDF physically strips the underlying text and vector paths from the compiled document and replaces them with solid pixel fills and legal exemption tags.

What are the build prerequisites?

Pre-compiled standalone binaries include their own self-contained runtime. If building directly from source, you only need the standard .NET 10 SDK.
